I meant to post this a few days ago, but the beginning of June found me at [iJS San Diego](https://javascript-conference.com/san-diego/) talking about React animations. The talk went well! I appreciate all the people who came out to hear me - I'll have a more formal conference writeup eventually.

For now, I want to summarize what the last month of writing about coreutils has done for me. Over the past month, I wanted to write every other weekday about a tool that I either use daily, or one I think I should know better. I ended up writing [11 posts]({{site_baseurl | relative_url}} {%link _projects/30-days-of-coreutils.markdown %}) about ~13 commands. (Strictly speaking, I should have gotten a 12th post out, but again, San Diego.)

They say that the best way to learn something is to teach it. I know that intellectually but it still shocks me how much more I learn when I set down to write about something. I feel that over the past month, I've become more willing/able to try chaining commands together in ways I haven't before, and just in general more confident on the command line.

I started the month already feeling fairly confident on the command line (usage breeds confidence, after all) but a month of thinking hard about at least a small percentage of the available coreutils out there has helped a lot. I recommend this challenge quite highly. :)
