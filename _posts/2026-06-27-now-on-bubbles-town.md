A few weeks ago I learned about a new project called bubbles.town that aims to collect and "bubble up" the best of the best of personal blogs. No businesses, no paywalls, no aggregators. It just subscribes to thousands of RSS feeds and throws them into a queue. The ones that get the most votes appear on a homepage. That's it.

I really love Bubbles.town's [RSS feeds](https://bubbles.town/rss). So far I'm only subscribed to the front page feed, but there are also feeds by category, by votes, by topic, and a new digest feature that seems like it could be interesting. 

The front page feed is a bit of a firehose (although much less so than the new feed, which contains EVERYTHING), and I definitely don't read it all, but I've discovered so many interesting reads. A few of my faves so far (not all are tech related):

- [.gitignore isn't the only way to ignore files in git](https://nelson.cloud/.gitignore-isnt-the-only-way-to-ignore-files-in-git/)
- [Where to find the colors your screen can't show you](https://moultano.wordpress.com/2026/06/19/where-to-find-the-colors-your-screen-cant-show-you/)
- [PSA for web designers: flags have ratios!](https://www.rubenerd.au/psa-for-web-designers-flags-have-ratios/)
- [The utter joy of the Spirit Crossing playtest](https://blog.curiousquail.com/the-utter-joy-of-the-spirit-crossing-playtest/)
- [the fivefold path to falling asleep](https://rosano.ca/blog/the-fivefold-path-to-falling-asleep/)

For the most part, the posts on bubbles.town, even the ones that make it to the front page, are not polished. I don't mean this in a bad way. They're personal, they're scattered, they respond to other people's posts by quoting and linking. There are book reviews, people doing "question challenges" that other bloggers challenged them to, funny photos of birds. It basically feels like the internet circa 2004, which we need a lot more of these days.

I wasn't sure if my blog, which has a tech-y focus and isn't a personal, confessional blog, would be a fit, but I emailed the founder and said I don't feel like I belong on the [angry orange site](https://news.ycombinator.com/), and my feed was added to the list of blogs. Easy! Now, if you like one of my posts, you can either upvote it from the feed, from my [blog's landing page](https://bubbles.town/source/rachelkaufman.dev), or right from the cute little widget at the bottom of each post. To vote, you need a Fediverse account, so if you were wondering what kind of person I am, now you're not.

I hope you enjoy Bubbles.town as much as I do.
