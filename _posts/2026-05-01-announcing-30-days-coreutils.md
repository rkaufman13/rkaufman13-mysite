I've been using the command line for a long time, but only seriously after I became a software developer in 2022. I would rate myself as reasonably comfortable with most of the 'daily' commands that I use, including many in the [GNU Core Utilities (coreutils)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_Core_Utilities) collection, but I am a complete novice at other things.

Inspired by [Jacob Tomlinson's 100 Days of Coreutils](https://jacobtomlinson.dev/blog/100-days-of-coreutils/) I'm setting myself a challenge for the month of May to try to learn a new coreutil command, or learn something new about my daily commands, every other day. I am not brave/motivated enough to cover all ~100 utils and I don't have the time to do one every day, but I think by the end of the month I'll have covered between 12 and 15 utils and hopefully learned something interesting.

Follow along during the month of May to see how I do!
