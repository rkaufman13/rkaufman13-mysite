(Backstory here: feel free to <a href="#slides">jump ahead</a> if you just want to see the slides.)

Last year and early this year, I was honored to contribute to an interactive at the [International Spy Museum](https://www.spymuseum.org/) that my husband pitched them for their [exhibit on camouflage](https://www.spymuseum.org/camouflage/). He designed the interactive and came up with its specs, and I contributed (some) to building the frontend. I learned a ton during the process and turned those learnings into a talk, which I've recently recorded for [React Summit](https://reactsummit.com/schedule) (and will be giving a longer version of at [iJS](https://javascript-conference.com/san-diego/) next month).

Given that, it seemed like the appropriate time to publish the slides. They're embedded below. This is the first "big" presentation I've done in [RevealJS](https://revealjs.com/) and it was a huge learning experience in itself, but I don't think I can ever go back to Google Slides.

I think these slides mostly speak for themselves, although there may be some jokes that "you had to be there" for.

Enjoy!
